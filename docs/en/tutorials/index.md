# Tutorials

Hands-on lessons, in order. Each one ends with something working.

| # | Lesson | You will learn | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | [Your first bot](first-bot.md) | Run Arcanum and a bot together, connect a number, answer questions automatically | 15 min |
| 2 | [Human takeover](human-takeover.md) | Let your team answer from the phone while the bot steps aside | 10 min |
| 3 | [Notify customers from your system](notifications.md) | Send order updates, invoices and reminders from your own code | 15 min |
| 4 | [Move to a new server](move-server.md) | Back up sessions and webhooks and restore them elsewhere, without new QR codes | 10 min |

**Before you start:** Docker installed, a phone with WhatsApp to act as the bot (a spare
number is best), and another phone to talk to it.

The code for lesson 1 lives in the repository:
[`examples/faq-bot`](https://github.com/rafaeldourado9/arcanum/tree/main/examples/faq-bot).
